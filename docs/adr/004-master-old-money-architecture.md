# ADR 004: Master Architecture for Old-Money Minimalist Portfolio

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-11-12
- **Deciders**: Nischal Basnet, Principal Architect Agent

---

## 1. Context & Problem Statement
The portfolio is designed as an immaculate personal brand asset merging high-end editorial quiet luxury (inspired by the David Whyte & Mosh Mohammed references) with implicit storytelling of the developer's core passions:
- **Tailoring & Fashion**: Understated proportions, hairline borders (`#E7E2D8`), refined pill buttons, ample whitespace, high-contrast serif typography.
- **Seasonality (Autumn & Winter)**: Automatic calendar-based ambient HTML5 canvas drifting amber/olive leaves or gentle snow particles.
- **AI & Systems Depth**: Production projects (**Clip Cuisine**, **Gym Management System**) showcased via editorial cards with slide-out architectural drawers.
- **Solo Leveling Subtlety**: Pure secret easter egg triggered by keyboard shortcut `M` or clicking a discreet footer watermark, transforming the environment into "Shadow Monarch Mode" (deep charcoal with soft shadow-aura depth).

---

## 2. Key Decisions

### A. Layout & Flow
1. **Single-Page Editorial Journey**:
   - `Navbar`: Minimalist brand title (`NISCHAL BASNET // ARCHITECT`), navigation anchors (`Work`, `Philosophy`, `Craft`, `Inquiry`), and an olive pill CTA (`Get in Touch`).
   - `Hero Section`: Split layout. Left: Cormorant Garamond display title, dynamic typewriter role rotator in olive green ("Crafting [Full-Stack Web Systems | AI & Vision Pipelines | Scalable Backend Services]"), bio, olive pill button. Right: Botanical watercolor autumn/winter motif with live status pill (`● Available for Engineering`).
   - `The Craft (Philosophy)`: Three Pillars triad (Architecture & Systems, Proportion & Form, The Atmosphere).
   - `Selected Works`: Editorial Case Cards with Slide-out Architectural Drawers for **Clip Cuisine** and **Gym Management System**.
   - `Technical Capabilities`: Minimalist Horizon Matrix (horizontal spec ledger with Swiss-dial counters `01 //`, `02 //`).
   - `Inquiry & Contact`: Minimalist form with quick-copy email button (`nischealbasnet10@gmail.com`).
   - `Footer`: Kathmandu time zone, seasonal status with toggle, copyright, and the secret Shadow Monarch watermark.

### B. Visual & Color Tokens
- **Base Background**: Warm Linen & Cashmere Cream (`#F9F6F0`).
- **Cards**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with diffuse luxury shadow (`rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.05)`).
- **Text**: Deep Espresso (`#1C1917`), Refined Charcoal (`#44403C`), Warm Stone (`#78716C`).
- **Signature Accent**: Olive Green (`#4A5D4E`).
- **Monarch / Shadow Mode**: Deep Espresso Charcoal (`#141312`) with warm alabaster text (`#F5F2EB`) and subtle cyan shadow glow (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`).

### C. Typography
- **Headings**: `Cormorant Garamond` (luxury editorial serif).
- **Body & UI**: `Inter` (clean readability).
- **Details & Coordinates**: `JetBrains Mono` (Swiss horology precision).

---

## 3. Consequences
- Zero gamified visual clutter on initial page load.
- Professional credibility for top-tier engineering roles and high-end clients.
- Rich, memorable interactions (drawers, seasonal canvas, hidden monarch mode) rewarding visitors who explore.
