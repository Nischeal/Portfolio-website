# Portfolio Domain Glossary & Concept Models

This document defines key terms, architectural concepts, and domain models used throughout **Nischal Basnet's** portfolio website.

---

## 🏛️ Domain Concepts & Mapping

| Concept | Domain Meaning | Portfolio Implementation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Editorial Split Hero** | The primary introduction establishing identity, technical breadth, and aesthetic restraint. | **Hero Section**: Cormorant Garamond display headline, dynamic olive typewriter rotator ("Crafting [Full-Stack Web Systems | AI & Vision Pipelines | Scalable Backend Services]"), paired with an artistic botanical card and live availability pill. |
| **Grounded About Section** | Authentic, conversational introduction to who Nischal is, what he builds, and his engineering approach. | `<AboutSection />`: Straightforward narrative on background (Kathmandu, Nepal), core technical interests (YOLO, Python, Laravel, databases), and craftsmanship mindset. |
| **Seasonal Canvas** | Ambient background layer reflecting love for Autumn and Winter seasons. | `<SeasonalCanvas />`: Zero-dependency HTML5 canvas rendering drifting amber/olive leaves (Autumn) or gentle soft snow (Winter). |
| **Monarch Mode** | Secret dark-mode shift revealing subtle Solo Leveling shadow essence. | `<ThemeToggle />`: Triggered by keyboard `M` or clicking the footer watermark, shifting the site into deep charcoal espresso with a faint cyan shadow aura. |
| **Selected Work** | Curated showcase of authentic engineering projects. | **Projects Section**: Editorial case cards presenting **Clip Cuisine** and **Gym Management System** with slide-out architectural drawers and direct GitHub source links. |
| **Technical Capabilities** | Categorized summary of engineering proficiencies. | **Horizon Matrix**: Horizontal spec ledger with Swiss-dial counters (`01 //`, `02 //`), mastery indicators, and tech tags. |
| **Inquiry Conduit** | Direct communication channel for recruiters and engineering collaborators. | **Contact Section**: Minimalist editorial form with quick-copy button for `nischealbasnet10@gmail.com`. |
